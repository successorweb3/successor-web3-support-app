# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, or religion.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and inclusive
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination
- Trolling, intimidation, or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking fair corrective action in response to any behavior that violates this code.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces related to the project, including GitHub discussions, issues, pull requests, and collaborative project spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations of this code may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
